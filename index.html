Decision Playground

2026, personal

If a small decision model’s confidence can be trusted, only the ambiguous cases need an LLM. This testbed checks that claim: a confidence gate routes between small models and Claude on ticket routing, elevator dispatch, and robot pick-and-place, all scored the same way.

  • Trained my own decider: Qwen3.5-2B with LoRA (0.33% of weights) and an option-scoring head, temperature-calibrated.
  • Compared it with Laya, Mica-4B, and Claude Haiku, plotting accuracy against the share of cases sent to the LLM.

Python, PyTorch, PEFT, Claude API, vanilla JS

Research

LGL-bench

With Prof. Tony Dear, Columbia

How should we measure strategic behavior in language agents? We use games whose best move is computable (Baskin-31, Op-Max) as measuring instruments, plus a three-player social-deduction game, and show how win rate, flat per-turn metrics, and simulated partners can each distort which model looks best.
